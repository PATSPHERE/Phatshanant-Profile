Executive Profile

20+ Years of Healthcare Leadership & Innovation

From the bedside to the boardroom — a career shaped by clinical discipline, strategic vision, digital transformation, and a belief that healthcare must remain profoundly human.

My journey began as a Registered Nurse — a foundation that gave me a deep, lived understanding of what healthcare truly means to a human being. That clinical empathy has guided every strategic decision I have made since.
